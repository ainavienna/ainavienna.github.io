City Walk Through Vienna

The start of autumn offered a respite from the scorching heat and was perfect for AINA members to discover Vienna through a walking tour titled “2000 years in 2 hours”!

This insightful tour was organised by our fellow AINA member Ian Banerjee, an architect and urban planner associated with TU Wien’s faculty of architecture and planning, who calls Vienna his home. As we weaved through the streets of the Innere Stadt (inner city), we marvelled as he pointed out the hidden details in the city’s architecture and urban planning.

The tour showed how Vienna evolved from the Roman era to today’s modern hub, and how crucial its geographical location and proximity to the Danube were to its development. It was fascinating to see how AINA members, owing to their professional backgrounds, knew different facets of Vienna. This made it an interactive walking tour that helped members learn from each other and forge connections.

Indian Film Festival 2026

March 2026 · Vienna

In March 2026, AINA members attended the Indian Film Festival organised by the Embassy of India in Vienna. The screenings offered a wonderful glimpse into the diversity of Indian cinema, and it was lovely to be part of another Embassy event while catching up with familiar faces from our growing community.

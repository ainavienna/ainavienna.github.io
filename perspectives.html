AINA at the United Nations: Exploring Diplomacy, Science and Careers in Vienna

6 March 2026 · Vienna International Centre

On 6 March 2026, some AINA members visited the Vienna International Centre (VIC), one of the four major UN headquarters and a hub for international diplomacy. The guided tour offered members an opportunity to explore the Centre and learn more about the work of Vienna-based UN organisations across areas including international security, sustainable development, the peaceful uses of nuclear energy, outer space and crime prevention.

The visit was followed by an informal dinner and conversation with Ms Crosby Plaza, who is part of the G-77 Vienna Chapter Secretariat. She shared insights from her experience in diplomacy and science diplomacy within the UN system.

The discussion brought together AINA members from diverse academic and professional backgrounds and explored how scientific expertise intersects with international policymaking, diplomacy and multilateral cooperation.

For AINA, the evening reflected our aim of creating opportunities not only to connect members with institutions and professionals in Austria, but also to foster conversations around career pathways, knowledge exchange and the India-Austria professional community. The combination of an institutional visit with an open, informal discussion made for an engaging evening of learning, reflection and connection.

Interaction with Dr. Pradeep Monga

AINA members gathered at the Strandcafe an der Alte Donau for a fruitful discussion on how to navigate career opportunities in international organisations like UN, UNIDO and IAEA with Dr. Pradeep Monga, the Senior Strategic Advisor to the Director General at UNIDO and Senior Advisor and Emeritus Visiting Scholar at IIASA.

During this insightful discussion, Dr. Monga led us through his journey and how the different opportunities and positions helped him become a Senior Energy, Climate and Sustainability Advisor to such international organisations.

We not only had a lively discussion on the structure of UN organisations along with the application and recruitment process but there was also special emphasis on the interpersonal and soft skills that prove to be crucial in such settings.

We are deeply grateful to Dr. Monga for taking out the time from his busy schedule to guide us!

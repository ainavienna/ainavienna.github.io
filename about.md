# About AINA

AINA  stands for Austria India Network Association and aims to bring
people together and create a welcoming community built on
connection, cultural exchange, and collaboration.

## Our Mission

To connect individuals and communities, encourage cultural
exchange, and create opportunities for networking, learning
and collaboration.

## Our Vision

To build an inclusive and vibrant network that strengthens
relationships between communities and supports meaningful
initiatives.

## Our Values

- **Community:** Creating a welcoming environment for everyone.
- **Connection:** Building relationships and networks.
- **Culture:** Encouraging cultural exchange and understanding.
- **Collaboration:** Supporting shared ideas and initiatives.
- **Inclusion:** Respecting different backgrounds and perspectives.

## Our Story

AINA's story is being written together with its community.
We look forward to sharing our activities, achievements, and
experiences as the association grows.

First Brunch

30 November 2025 | Café Alt Wien, Vienna

On 30 November 2025, AINA held its first brunch meeting at Café Alt Wien, one of Vienna’s traditional coffee houses. The gathering was relaxed and informal, bringing together a wonderfully diverse group of Indians living in Vienna, from people settled here for many years with their families to young interns and students working at various organisations. Over a leisurely brunch, conversations flowed easily across age groups and backgrounds. This first meeting laid the foundation for the gatherings that followed and set the friendly, welcoming tone that has defined our network ever since.

Christmas Market Hopping

19 December 2024 | Spittelberg and Rathausplatz, Vienna

On 19 December 2024, some AINA members came together for an evening exploring two of Vienna’s much-loved Christmas markets. We began in the neighbourhood of Spittelberg, walking through its narrow streets filled with the aromas of baking and Glühwein, and browsing the market’s stalls filled with handcrafted gifts, woollen accessories, ceramics, glassware and other festive finds before making our way towards Rathausplatz.

The walk between the two markets gave members time to chat, catch up, and enjoy Vienna in its winter setting. At Rathausplatz, we continued the evening among the lights, seasonal stalls, and lively atmosphere of one of the city’s most recognisable Christmas markets.

The evening was deliberately simple: a chance to step away from work and research, meet fellow members outside a formal setting, and enjoy a shared experience in the city we call home. For AINA, such gatherings are an important part of building a community that extends beyond professional connections, creating space for friendships, conversations, and a sense of belonging among members of the India-Austria community.

Continuing the spirit of our friendly meet-ups, AINA celebrated India’s Independence Day spirit with a potluck hosted by Dr. SenGupta at his apartment. Members brought a variety of dishes, creating a colourful spread that sparked lively conversations about India’s rich culinary diversity. Over food, we also discussed professional opportunities, networking and the growing ties between India and Austria.

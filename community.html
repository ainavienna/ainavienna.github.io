COMMUNITY

First Brunch

30 November 2025 | Café Alt Wien, Vienna

On 30 November 2025, AINA held its first brunch meeting at Café Alt Wien, one of Vienna’s traditional coffee houses. The gathering was relaxed and informal, bringing together a wonderfully diverse group of Indians living in Vienna, from people settled here for many years with their families to young interns and students working at various organisations. Over a leisurely brunch, conversations flowed easily across age groups and backgrounds. This first meeting laid the foundation for the gatherings that followed and set the friendly, welcoming tone that has defined our network ever since.

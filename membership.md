# Become a Member

We welcome people who are interested in connecting, participating
and contributing to the AINA community.

## Why join AINA?

- Connect with people and expand your network.
- Participate in community and cultural activities.
- Discover opportunities for collaboration.
- Share ideas and contribute to initiatives.
- Stay informed about events and announcements.

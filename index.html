WHO WE ARE

About AINA

The Austria India Network Association (AINA) is a community network dedicated to bringing people together and fostering meaningful connections between Austria and India.

We aim to create opportunities for cultural exchange, networking, friendship and collaboration, while celebrating the diversity and shared experiences of our communities.

Our Mission

To connect people, encourage cultural understanding, strengthen community relationships and create opportunities for meaningful engagement between Austria and India.

What We Do

Community

Bringing people together and building a welcoming network through shared experiences.

Austria–India Network Visits the Vienna BioCenter for the Long Night of Research

24 April 2026 · Vienna

Members of the Austria–India Network Association joined us at the Vienna BioCenter for the Long Night of Research (Lange Nacht der Forschung) on 24 April 2026, turning the evening into a fun and engaging team activity.

The visit began at Vienna BioCenter, where a team of researchers demonstrated fluorescence microscopy and explained how researchers use light and fluorescent labels to visualise structures and processes inside living cells.

From there, we were guided on a tour of the Vienna BioCenter campus, including the University of Vienna Biology Building (UBB) and the Institute of Molecular Biotechnology (IMBA). Along the way, members explored the open labs and took part in a variety of hands-on experiments and science stalls prepared by researchers from across the campus.

These activities offered a glimpse into the diverse research happening at one of Europe’s leading life-science hubs, and sparked lively conversations about science, research careers, and life in Vienna.

The evening was a wonderful opportunity to connect members of our network with the scientific community in Vienna, and we look forward to organising more such activities together in the future.

TU India Science Day

20 March 2026 · TU Wien, Vienna

On 20 March 2026, several members of the Austria–India Network Association (AINA) attended the first India Science Day at TU Wien, held in the university’s Festsaal. Organised a few weeks after India’s National Science Day, the event brought together more than 160 participants from research, politics and diplomacy, including H.E. Ambassador Shambhu S. Kumaran, to celebrate and strengthen scientific cooperation between Austria and India.

A special highlight for our network was the inaugural WISSEN Awards (Women in Scientific Studies to Empower Nations), initiated by the Embassy of India. AINA member Pallavi Deolal was among the very first awardees.

The programme featured showcases of Indo-Austrian research projects, funding information and panel discussions on research collaboration and start-ups. The day ended with lively networking, where AINA members connected with the wider scientific community and with TU Wien students and researchers who are part of our network.
